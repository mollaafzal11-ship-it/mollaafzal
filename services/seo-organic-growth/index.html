SEO & organic growth

Improve how your website is understood by search engines and how easily potential customers find the pages that answer their questions.

SEO work starts with understanding the website, the search landscape and the pages most likely to support business goals. Priorities are then organized into practical actions rather than disconnected tasks.

01

What this can include

  • Keyword research and search-intent mapping
  • On-page titles, meta descriptions, headings and content recommendations
  • Internal linking and content structure improvements
  • Technical SEO checks for crawlability, indexability, canonical tags and sitemap coverage
  • Competitor and content-gap analysis
  • Google Search Console performance reviews
02

How I approach the work

  • Review the current website and available search data
  • Identify issues and opportunities by impact and effort
  • Prepare a prioritized action plan
  • Implement agreed updates or coordinate with the developer
  • Measure changes using Search Console and analytics data
